Here are examples as ready-to-paste endings for the set-up prompt:

> "Set up an agent board here using https://github.com/stefanolafsson/agent-board. I want agents that …"

**Small businesses and shops**
- **Online shop:** …read each day's new orders and reviews, spot recurring complaints, and send me a short note on what customers keep mentioning.
- **Restaurant or café:** …compare my suppliers' latest prices with my recipes every week and tell me which dishes are losing money and how to fix them.
- **Quotes for customers:** …turn each customer request into a draft quote by estimating materials, labour and transport separately, then check it against my past jobs.
- **Supplier watch:** …watch my suppliers' price lists and delivery news and warn me when prices rise or deliveries slip, with alternatives.

**Offices and companies**
- **New policy rollout:** …read every new internal policy, work out which departments it affects, check each department's documents against it, and draft each department's response.
- **Customer support:** …sort incoming support tickets, draft a reply to each one following our reply rules, and check the drafts before I send them.
- **Sales follow-up:** …summarize my notes after each sales meeting, draft a follow-up email, and remind me if the customer hasn't replied within a week.
- **Hiring:** …screen each job application against our criteria and give me a shortlist with the reasons. I make the final decision.
- **Contracts:** …review each contract I receive against our standard terms, flag the risky clauses, and summarize it in plain language with a recommendation.
- **Monthly reporting:** …analyse our monthly exports from accounting, sales and support and draft one report on the three most important changes.

**Nonprofits, clubs and municipalities**
- **Sports club:** …check registrations and payments and draft reminders to members who haven't paid or are missing forms.
- **Volunteer scheduling:** …build the volunteer rota from everyone's availability and point out gaps and people who are scheduled too often.
- **Municipal case handling:** …classify incoming citizen requests, find the rules that apply, and draft a reply, noting which department should handle it.
- **Funding for nonprofits:** …find foundation calls every week that fit our organisation and draft an application for each good match.

**Communication and media**
- **Newsletter:** …collect the week's news on our topics, write a newsletter draft, edit it to our style guide, and fact-check it before I send it.
- **Social media:** …monitor mentions of our brand, draft replies to the negative ones, and send me a weekly summary of how people talk about us.
- **Translation:** …translate my documents, back-translate them to catch errors, and check the terminology against our glossary.
- **Press releases:** …critique my press release drafts as a journalist would, check legal risks and tone, and suggest a better version.

**IT and operations**
- **Error triage:** …read new error logs and alerts, work out the likely cause, and suggest a fix.
- **Security updates:** …compare new security vulnerability notices with the software we use and tell me which ones to patch first.
- **Data quality:** …check our nightly data export and tell me only when something looks wrong, and why.

**At home and personal**
- **Big purchases:** …research the options when I'm buying something big, compare them, and recommend the one that fits my budget and needs.
- **Travel:** …research each destination of a trip I'm planning, build an itinerary, and check it fits my budget.
- **Household admin:** …sort my scanned bills and letters, keep a list of deadlines, and warn me before renewals with cheaper alternatives.
- **Learning a language or skill:** …give me a lesson and exercises every week, quiz me, and adjust to how I'm doing.
- **Job hunting:** …find new job ads every day that match my profile and draft a tailored cover letter for the best ones.
